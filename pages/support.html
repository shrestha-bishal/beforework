Made carefully, maintained independently

Beforework began with a simple belief: getting organized should make life feel lighter, not louder.

I wanted to build a quieter place to gather the moving pieces of a day, find the next step, and get back to the work that matters. If Beforework has helped you do that, thank you. Knowing it has become part of someone's day means a great deal.

Your work stays yours

Beforework is open source and local-first. Your workspace lives in a JSON file you choose and keep on your device. Support helps keep Beforework free of ads and subscriptions; a donation is never required to use the app.

What your support helps sustain

  • File handling and compatibility fixes as browsers evolve
  • Keyboard access, screen-reader labels, and clear documentation
  • Privacy-focused features and ongoing maintenance
